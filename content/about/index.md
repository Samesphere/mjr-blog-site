## I make AI easy for small business

If you run a small business and know you should be doing something with AI, but keep putting it off because it all feels too hard, you're exactly who I like working with.

### The tech wizard behind the curtain

I come in, sit with you and your team, and learn how the work gets done today. Then I re-engineer the messy, repetitive parts into systems that run themselves, so your people can spend their time on the things that really do need a human.

### What working with me looks like

- Your staff brain dump everything that feels repetitive, manual, boring or annoying, so they feel heard and excited about what's possible.
- Honest advice on what's worth automating, what isn't, and why.
- I document your processes and design solutions with your team's input and feedback, so they own the outcome.
- I train your staff to delegate everyday tasks to an AI team member, so admin and other roles get hours back each week.

Imagine data entry, chasing emails, copying between systems and rebuilding the same weekly report, plus whatever else your team flags, all happening automatically.

### Proof it works

I've been mapping and documenting business processes since 2006 and building automated workflows since 2010, mostly in IT support and project delivery. In my most recent role at a Gold Coast manufacturer I automated parts of payroll, bank reconciliation, debt collection and sales reporting.

### The practical bits

I'm an Australian citizen living in Southport on the Gold Coast, with my own transport, open to 3 to 4 days a week of part-time, on-site work.
